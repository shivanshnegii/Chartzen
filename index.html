About Chartzen

Chartzen is a charting tool for finance professionals: analysts, accountants, FP&A teams and business owners who keep their numbers in Excel and need clear charts for reports, reviews and presentations.

Instead of building every chart by hand, you upload your data and get a working set of charts in seconds, then refine them to match what you need.

  • Monthly and quarterly revenue and expense trends
  • Budget versus actual comparisons
  • Region, product or cost-centre breakdowns
  • Quick charts for board decks and management reports

Questions people ask

Which file formats work?

Excel files (.xlsx and .xls) and CSV files. If a workbook has several sheets, choose the one you want from the Sheet menu.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Everything is read and charted inside your own browser. Your file is not uploaded or stored anywhere.

Why is one of my columns not available to plot?

A column is treated as numeric when most of its cells are numbers. Columns with mostly text are used as the X axis instead. Check for stray text in your number columns.

How large can my file be?

Thousands of rows work smoothly. The data preview shows the first 50 rows, but every row is used in the charts and totals.

Can I use the charts in a report or deck?

Yes. Use the Download menu on any chart for PNG, JPG, PDF or CSV, or export every chart at once as a PDF report or Excel workbook.
